# Future layer, not in FreshTrack yet: farmers markets and farm stands

Source: farmers market research completed October 30, 2026, after the project pivoted to pantries and soup kitchens. Saved here so a seasonal market layer can be added back later without redoing the research. Do not publish this list without re-checking each market, because most 2026 outdoor seasons had ended by late October, and several addresses and hours below are marked uncertain. Geocode every address with Amazon Location Place Index before adding it to data/places.geojson.

- Kalamazoo Farmers Market, seasonal, 1204 Bank St, Kalamazoo, MI 49001. Saturdays, May through November, 7am-1pm; Tuesdays and Thursdays, June through October, 8am-1pm. Weekday market ended for 2026 on Oct 8, 2026, per Farm to Table Growers; indoor winter market listed as Saturdays, Dec 5, 2026-Apr 24, 2027, except Dec 26, 8am-1pm. Sources: Kalamazoo city market page, Farm to Table Growers.
- Portage Zhang Farmers Market, seasonal, address direction uncertain: 125 East Centre Ave, Portage, MI 49002, per City of Portage and Farm to Table Growers. Uncertain: an Encore Magazine 2026 preview described the new location as 125 W. Centre Ave, just west of the Portage Zhang Senior Center. Do not use the W. version without re-checking the official page. Sundays, 9am-1pm; 2026 season May 3-Oct 11, 2026. Official page states closed for the season, returning May 2027. Source: City of Portage farmers market page.
- People's Food Co-op / PFC Natural Grocery & Deli, year-round co-op, not a seasonal market, 507 Harrison St, Kalamazoo, MI 49007. Daily 8am-8pm per PFC's own site, updated 2026-09-21. Uncertain: some third-party listings show 7am-9pm or 8am-9pm.
- Texas Township Farmers' Market ("Fresh on Q"), seasonal, 7110 West Q Ave, Kalamazoo, MI 49009. Saturdays, May 9-Oct 17, 2026, 8am-noon; Tuesdays, June 2-Aug 25, 2026, 4pm-7pm; Holiday Market Nov 21, 8am-noon. Source: Texas Township farmers market page.
- Vicksburg Farmers' Market, seasonal, 300 N. Richardson St, Vicksburg, MI 49097. Fridays, May 22-Oct 2, 2026, 2pm-6pm. Farm to Table Growers also lists a one-day Winter Market Dec 12, 2026, uncertain, not confirmed on the market's own site snippet.
- Richland Farmers' Market, seasonal, 9400 East CD Ave, Richland, MI 49083, at Richland Area Community Center. Wednesdays, 3pm-6pm; opening day May 20, 2026; May-September. Exact 2026 end date uncertain: described as May-September / mid-September. Older listings show a different address, 9550 East M-89, do not use that for 2026.
- Comstock Township Farmers Market, seasonal, 5817 Comstock Ave, Kalamazoo, MI 49048, at Merrill Park. Mondays, 3pm-6pm, June 1-Sept 21, 2026; no market Labor Day, Sept 7, 2026.
- Gull Meadow Farms, seasonal farm market / U-pick, 8544 Gull Rd, Richland, MI 49083. Market hours Mon-Wed 9am-7pm, Thu-Sat 9am-8pm, Sun 10am-6pm; fall season listed as Sept 5-Oct 31, 2026. Activity hours differ from market hours.
- Husted's Farm Market & Cider Mill, seasonal, 9191 West Main, Kalamazoo, MI 49009. June-October, daily, 9am-7pm per Michigan Life listing; November, 9am-5pm. Uncertain: another listing gives June-October as 9am-6pm daily and November as 10am-5pm.
- Long Valley Farm, uncertain as a walk-in farm stand, 8350 East O Ave, Kalamazoo, MI 49048. Walk-in farm-stand hours not verified. Verified as a CSA farm and a vendor at Kalamazoo Farmers Market; do not assume the farm address is open to walk-in shoppers without checking.

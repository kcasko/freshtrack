FreshTrack

Food bank and mobile pantry access routing for Kalamazoo County. Find the nearest place to get groceries or a hot meal, filter by what you need, and get a route from where you are.

Demo basemap is showing. Add your Amazon Location Service API key in config.js to switch to the Amazon Location basemap, Place Index search, and driving or walking routes. See README for the exact setup.

Call before you go. Pantry and meal schedules change, and some sites only run on certain days of the month. If a card says hours differ between sources, trust the phone call, not the map.

Nearest places

Starting point: downtown Kalamazoo. Click anywhere on the map, use your location, or type an address and choose Find nearest 3.

Data date: October 30, 2026. Sources are listed in each place card and in data/places.geojson. Primary local sources: South Michigan Food Bank Kalamazoo County Resource Guide, Kalamazoo Loaves & Fishes, and local resource directories. Future dataset layers named in the submission: Feeding America agency data, USDA Food Access Research Atlas, and Michigan Open Data emergency food sites, to be added only after their licenses and tract-level files are checked.

How to read this map: emergency pantries give groceries to take home. Soup kitchens serve hot meals. Senior help and mobile distribution are programs, not always a fixed warehouse: Kalamazoo Loaves & Fishes runs senior commodities at community centers and residential sites, and mobile distributions at sites around the county.
